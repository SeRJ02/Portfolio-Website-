DumbMoney.in + MCP Server

Built a coupon site in 2 weeks. Then built a bot to maintain it. Still running.

Situation

The affiliate coupon space is dominated by incumbents with domain authority built over years. Getting organic traction without a paid budget means finding the gaps they've missed — specific sale keywords, niche retailer coupons, long-tail searches the big players don't bother optimising for. The other problem nobody talks about: coupon sites rot. Codes expire, retailers pause, offers change. Manually checking 200+ coupons across 40 retailers is a part-time job that directly eats into the time needed to grow the site.

Task

Build a revenue-generating affiliate coupon site from scratch with zero ad spend — purely on organic SEO. Then build the tooling to maintain it without the maintenance killing the momentum.

Action

Two weeks from idea to live. Built dumbmoney.in with a clear SEO-first architecture — keyword research via Google Search Console, FAQ schema markup on every page, structured blog content targeting specific sale keywords and retailer coupons, and internal linking designed to pass authority across pages. Launched with 40 retailers and 200+ coupons. No paid promotion, no social push. Just search. Two weeks in, manually checking expired coupons was eating more time than writing new content. Built the dumbmoney-mcp server in 2 days. Deployed on Cloudflare Workers and published to the MCP registry. The server surfaces all existing coupon and retailer data from the site via typed tool endpoints — giving instant visibility into what's live without manually opening every page.

Result

  • Ranked #3 for target keyword in week one
  • 3,000+ impressions at 20% CTR with zero ad spend
  • ~1,000 visitors a day, growing organically
  • 200+ coupons across 40 retailers
  • Manual coupon verification effort cut by 50%
  • Still live, still growing

What I'd Do Differently

Started with Google Search Console which works, but has a chicken-and-egg problem — you need traffic to get data, and you need data to get traffic. I'd start with a proper keyword research tool earlier to front-load the right content decisions before launch. Two weeks was fast. A third week of research upfront would have saved a month of fixing the wrong bets.
